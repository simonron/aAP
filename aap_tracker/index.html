Adapted Autonomic Profile (aAP)

aAP Instructions

How to take a reading

  1. Use the diary at the specified times through the day and record the time for each reading.
  2. The Position drop-down is pre-set to the position specified by the diary form for that particular reading. It can be changed if necessary.
  3. Record blood pressure and heart rate, together with any symptoms you experience.
  4. Where the diary asks for a change of position, remain in the new position for the specified period before taking the reading.
  5. For activity/exertion entries, record the activity and take the requested readings before and after it.
  6. Record relevant food, fluid, activity and symptom information in the text areas. These expand as you type.

Recording the day

  • Complete readings on waking, around meals, during the morning and afternoon activity/exertion periods, and before sleeping.
  • Use the additional activity/symptom rows for significant events or symptoms not covered elsewhere.
  • Times are initially filled with the time the day's entry is created. Every time remains editable, including after the record has been saved and reloaded.
  • Save the record at any time. Saved days can be reloaded and edited.
  • Create PDF produces the PDF for the currently selected day only.

Safety

Follow the instructions supplied by your clinical team. If standing or exertion makes you feel unsafe, stop and sit or lie down rather than risking a fall.

Using your equipment

Take blood-pressure and heart-rate readings consistently with the monitor and technique specified by your clinical team. Enter the blood pressure as systolic/diastolic (for example 120/80) and enter heart rate separately.
